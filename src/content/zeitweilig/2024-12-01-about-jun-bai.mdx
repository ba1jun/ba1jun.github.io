I received my Ph.D. degree from the School of Computer Science and Engineering at Beihang University, China. I completed my doctoral studies under the supervision of [Prof. Wenge Rong](https://wgrong.github.io/) and [Prof. Chuantao Yin](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=_6JexHoAAAAJ).

My research primarily focuses on building **Trustworthy AI** systems. My current research themes include:

- **Belief memory in agents**: learning, retrieving, and revising beliefs over time.
- **Improving LLMs' faithful use of context** for evidence-grounded generation and reasoning.
- **Interpretability of LLMs and agents**: explaining internal reasoning, memory use, and decision-making.

I am always looking for **self-motivated interns**. Please feel free to drop me an email if interested.
